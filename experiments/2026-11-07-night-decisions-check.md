## Bugs found and fixed (each has a test, each its own commit)
1. **Teach / quiz answers were empty.** The study coach spread the tutor's own `mode` ("teach", "quiz") over its result, and `jobs.js` only reads `answer` for mode `study`. So "teach me the next Calculus lecture" ended as "Something went wrong: the model sent back an empty answer", every time. The coach now always says mode `study` (the tutor's mode is kept in `study.mode`).
2. **"Teach me the next lecture" taught lecture 1** to someone who had said they were at lecture 5. Now `teach` picks the first open lecture AFTER the stored place. Quiz is unchanged (earliest open one).
3. **A question cut off by a restart was lost silently** (no answer, no working card, nothing). At start-up, any question of the last 24 hours with no answer (not imported, answer not deleted) gets one plain reply: "Bot was restarted (or stopped) before it finished this message...". `markUnanswered` in `thread-store.js`.
4. **Study page did not match the real `GET /api/study`** (it read `courseId`, `held`, `youAre`...). It now reads `shouldBe {held,lastLecture,nextLecture}`, `you {positionKnown, position.seenThrough}`, `behind`, `questions`. The test uses an object copied from the running server.
5. **Yes / No proposal buttons sent plain words.** They now call `POST /api/study/confirm` and `/cancel` with the card id (single use; a stale card says "no longer waiting"). Tested with the real page: an assignment card saved, a lecture-material card attached.
6. **Daily-summary suggestions had no place on the page** (the routes existed). A "Bot noticed something in your earlier days" box now shows each fact/task with the quote, buttons Remember it / Remind me / No. Tested: No dismissed one, Remember it saved one into memory.
7. **Restore only worked right after delete.** New `GET /api/thread/trash` and a "Deleted messages" button with a list and Restore; tested after a reload.
8. **"Wrong? Ask again normally"** now sends a real `route: "general"` on `/api/ask` (only that value is accepted, anything else is a 400). The server skips course, calendar, addEvent and recall and answers as a plain question. The visible sentence in front of the question is gone.
9. **Step text** now says what is happening: `[study] teach` -> "Preparing the lesson (Calculus 2)", `[tutor] asking` -> "The tutor model is writing (this can take 20 seconds)", course answers -> "Reading Calculus 2, lecture 5 (4 pages)" (the lecture comes from the question; `study.js` writes it in the log line).
10. **Reply about course chats not being searched** is now correct for the thread: "I could not find that in your conversation, including your course questions. (Calendar replies and the daily brief are not searched.)". With the switch off the old sentence stays. (Careful: a test forbids the word "morning" in core.js.)
11. **The desk sent "explain how Dijkstra works" to `teach`**, and the tutor then refused ("I have nothing about the lecture of Sunday 18 Oct"). Both briefings now say teach is for a LECTURE of the timetable; a question about one concept is `ask`. Re-run: answered, labelled "Outside your files".
