# 2026-11-15: Gemini 4 Hydrogen, can Bot use it and is it useful?

## What Hydrogen is

- **Gemini 4 Hydrogen**, released 2026-11-12: Google's fast, cheaper Gemini 4 model for agent work and everyday tasks. It replaces Gemini 3.8 Flash in the Gemini app and Search.
- Google's numbers (not checked by us): 51 on the Artificial Analysis index at its default "high" effort (Argon: 50), 2.5x faster than 3.8 Flash. API price $1.50 / $7.50 per million tokens in / out.
- In Antigravity for Pro and Ultra.
- Sources: [Google blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-hydrogen/), [gHacks](https://www.ghacks.net/2026/11/13/google-releases-gemini-4-hydrogen-in-search-and-the-gemini-app-replacing-gemini-3-8-flash/), [TestingCatalog](https://www.testingcatalog.com/google-launches-gemini-4-hydrogen-for-pro-and-ultra-users/), [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/google-launches-gemini-4-hydrogen-a-faster-smarter-pricier-flash-successor).

## In agy on this PC (agy 1.5.8, Google AI Pro)

- `agy models` lists `gemini-4-hydrogen-low`, `-medium`, `-high` and `-xhigh`.
- `/usage` has no Hydrogen row. Measured: 9 Hydrogen calls moved **Gemini Models** (5-hour window 100% -> 99%) and did not move Argon's week. So **Hydrogen spends the same pool as Flash**, which was at 99% for the week.
- Added to `router.js` as `hydrogenLow` and `hydrogenMedium` (pool `GEMINI`). They are not in any chain.

## The trial (`experiments/hydrogen-trial.js`, results in `hydrogen-results.json`)

- The same 6 made-up messages were sent in the brain's real prompt (brain-brief.md plus a made-up context, slim agent).
- The photo is `hydrogen-photo.png`, an invented note: "lecture moved to Wednesday 14:00-16:00, Building 90 hall 123, Quiz 2 on Dec 3".
- 21 calls in all.

| model | avg time (5 text tests) | photo time | tokens per text call | photo tokens | math | C++ | tool CALL | Hebrew | photo card | leaks |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hydrogen low | **9.2 s** | **13.6 s** | ~8,500 (7.7k in, 100-500 thinking) | 33,900 | right (1, 1/2) | right | valid | yes | valid, but see below | none |
| Hydrogen medium (3 tests) | 9.1 s | - | ~9,000 | - | right, clearer steps | - | valid | yes | - | none |
| Argon low | 11.3 s | 17.8 s | ~7,400 (0 thinking) | 31,200 | right | right, best example | valid | yes | one card, one-time event | none |
| Flash medium | 11.9 s (one 18.9 s) | **66.8 s** | ~8,400 (chat: 11k) | 49,100 | right | right | valid | yes | one card, one-time event | none |

What I checked by hand:
- **Math:** all three got ∫₀^{π/2} x·sin x dx = 1 and lim (1−cos x)/x² = 1/2, with correct steps. That is 2 problems only, so it does not lift the "Flash made math errors" worry.
- **Tool use:** every model wrote `CALL {"tool":"set_reminder",...,"when":"2026-11-16T09:00"}` (the right day and time) plus a short reply under it. None leaked the prompt (`leaksPrompt` was false everywhere).
- **Hebrew:** all replies were in Hebrew, with technical terms left in English, and all ended with one check question, as the brief asks.
- **Photo judgement (the one real difference):** "Lecture moved, new time Wednesday" is unclear: one week or for good?
  - Argon and Flash made ONE card with a one-time event on Wed 2026-11-18 (Flash also asked about making it weekly).
  - Hydrogen low made TWO add_events cards. One was a weekly series "until 2027-01-31", an end date it made up. Code would accept it (the format is valid), so Omri would have to catch it on the card.
- **Style:** Hydrogen low is the shortest, and does not always ask a follow-up in plain chat. Argon low's answers were a bit richer (a fuller C++ example, a question back).

Quota spent by the whole trial: Gemini Models week 99% -> 98% (5-hour 100% -> 99%), Argon week 62% -> 61%, Claude untouched.

## Recommendation (Omri decides; no live chain was changed)

1. **Replace Flash with Hydrogen where Flash is a helper.** It uses the same pool and is at least as fast, much faster and cheaper on photos (13.6 s and 34k tokens, against 66.8 s and 49k).
   - Candidates: the photo fallback (`Flash medium` -> `Hydrogen low`), the daily summary and position readers (Flash high/medium), and crosslang (Flash low).
   - Code checks the output of all of these already, so the risk is small.
2. **Brain: worth a trial as the 2nd model, before Sonnet-in-agy.** The "Claude and GPT models" pool is at 7% for the week, while "Gemini Models" is almost unused (98%).
   - Possible chain: Argon low -> **Hydrogen low** -> Sonnet low in agy -> Claude Code.
   - Before that, run a bigger math check (about 10 calculus or linear-algebra problems with known answers), because of the Flash rule.
   - Also add one line to the brief about unclear photos: "if it is unclear whether an event repeats, make it one-time and ask". Hydrogen guessed a weekly series with an end date it made up.
3. **Keep Argon first** for the brain, the tutor (Argon-only rule) and PDF reading. Hydrogen was not tested on reading big PDFs.
4. `timeoutFor` has no Hydrogen case, so it gets the 3-minute default. That is fine, since every call took under 15 s.
