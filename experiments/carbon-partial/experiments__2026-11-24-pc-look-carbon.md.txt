# PC Look — Build Summary (Gemini 4 Carbon, Improve Bot)

Built on 2026-11-24 from `experiments/2026-11-24-pc-look-plan.md`.

## What was built

- **`pc-look.js`:** core PC Look module (Node built-ins only).
  - **Open windows & screenshots:** runs PowerShell directly via `execFile("powershell.exe", ["-NoProfile", "-NonInteractive", "-Command", ...], { windowsHide: true })` (no shell, fixed constant scripts, `.NET` `EnumWindows` / `IsIconic` / `DwmGetWindowAttribute` / `PrintWindow` only; resolves UWP `ApplicationFrameHost` children such as `SystemSettings.exe`). Never focuses, moves, unminimizes, clicks or types.
  - **Chrome tabs:** reads open tabs (`tabs`), tab page text (`tab_text`) and active-tab screenshots (`tab_shot`) through `moodle-bridge.js` (`KINDS` extended with whole-number-only arguments; `tab*` jobs never alter Moodle's `loggedIn` state).
  - **Allowed lists (`data/pc-look.json`):** separate `sites` (e.g. `moodle.bgu.ac.il`) and `apps` (e.g. `Code.exe`) lists, written atomically. `pc_allow` adds an item only when Omri wrote that site or app name in his own message (checked by `siteInText` / `appInText`) and never after web/Moodle/photo/PC/file reads or file follow-ups. Looking at an unlisted target produces a single-use 15-minute **Confirm card** with **"Just this once"**, **"Always allow (add to the list)"** and **"Cancel"**.
  - **Safety rules in code:**
    - `NEVER_ALLOWED` blocks password managers (`1Password`, `Bitwarden`, `KeePass`, `KeePassXC`, `LastPass`, `Dashlane`, `NordPass`, `passwords.google.com`, browser password pages), `chrome://`, `edge://`, `about:`, `file://`, and banking sites/apps/windows — when adding to a list, before showing a card, and again on live tab URL / window title at look time (so a tab navigating mid-message is also caught).
    - Targets are picked strictly by **number** from the last `chrome_tabs` or `pc_windows` list (kept 2 hours).
    - Limits enforced in code: **3 looks per message**, **30 looks per hour**, **8 MB max** screenshot size with real **JPEG/PNG magic-byte** check.
    - Never runs on a timer, never in the morning brief or summaries, and refused in file-upload follow-ups (`options.attached`).
    - Every look is logged in `data/bot.log` (site or app name only, never page text or image content) and stated in Bot's reply (guaranteed by code if the model omits it; notes "on your PC" when asked from the phone).
- **`brain.js` & `brain-brief.md`:** added lookups `pc_windows`, `chrome_tabs` and doing-tools `pc_look`, `pc_allow`. After `pc_windows`, `chrome_tabs` or `pc_look` runs, `ctx.sawPc` triggers the same doing-tool refusals as a web page read (`afterWeb`). Picture looks switch the round to `photoChain` (`agy`) in a private temp folder under fixed names (`photo.png`, ...) and delete the temporary screenshot files in `finally`.
- **`server.js`, `jobs.js`, `preview.js` & `public/index.html`:** added `/api/pc-look`, `/api/pc-look/remove` and `/api/pc-look/decide` (works on PC and phone; tapping "Just this once" or "Always allow" runs the look on the PC and posts Bot's answer under Omri's question), the 3-button Confirm card (`textContent` only), and collapsible Allowed sites / Allowed apps lists in the sidebar with a **Remove** button per entry.
- **Tests (`tests/pc-look.test.js`, `tests/refusal-rules.test.js`, `tests/brain-brief.test.js`):** added full fake-only coverage for window parsing, PowerShell arguments, bridge & extension jobs, `NEVER_ALLOWED`, 2-hour list numbers & tab-navigation races, card choices, rate/image limits, temp-file cleanup, refusal matrix across `pc_windows` / `chrome_tabs` / `pc_look`, and page markup.

## What Omri must do by hand

1. Copy the updated `manifest.json` and `background.js` from `experiments/pc-look-extension.md` into `moodle-extension/`.
2. Open `chrome://extensions` in Chrome, click **Reload** on **Bot Moodle reader**, and accept the new permissions (`tabs`, `scripting`, `http://*/*`, `https://*/*`) once.
3. Restart Bot (`Restart Bot` button) so the new server routes and brain tools load.

## What was not changed directly and why

- **`moodle-extension/` and `.ps1` files** are guard files in Improve Bot (`selfedit-guard.js` rejects any edit to them). Following the spec's instructions for Improve Bot:
  - The exact Chrome extension code (`manifest.json` and `background.js`) was written to `experiments/pc-look-extension.md` (and verified in `tests/pc-look.test.js` against a fake `chrome` API), while `moodle-bridge.js` already supports `tabs`, `tab_text` and `tab_shot`.
  - PowerShell is invoked directly from `pc-look.js` (`execFile("powershell.exe", ["-NoProfile", "-NonInteractive", "-Command", ...])`) with constant scripts and handles passed via environment variables instead of a separate `.ps1` file.
- **`CLAUDE.md`** was left untouched as instructed; proposed bullet below.

## Proposed `CLAUDE.md` bullet

- **PC LOOK (2026-11-24, `pc-look.js`, `moodle-bridge.js`, `moodle-extension/`):** Bot can list open Chrome tabs (`chrome_tabs`: title and address via the unpacked extension) and open PC windows (`pc_windows`: program name and title via `.NET` `EnumWindows` in `powershell -NoProfile`, no shell), and look at ONE tab (`pc_look`, text or `captureVisibleTab` picture when active and non-minimized) or ONE window (`PrintWindow` picture), picked ONLY by number from the last list (kept 2 hours). Two allowed lists in `data/pc-look.json` (sites like `moodle.bgu.ac.il`, apps like `Code.exe`), shown in the sidebar with Remove buttons; changed ONLY when Omri wrote the name in his own message (`pc_allow`, refused after web/Moodle/photo/PC/file reads) or tapped "Always allow" on the 3-choice Confirm card ("Just this once", "Always allow (add to the list)", "Cancel", 15 min, single-use). Password managers, `chrome://`/`edge://`/`about:`/`file://` and banking (`NEVER_ALLOWED`) are refused always, even on a list or at look time if a tab navigated. Titles and page text return inside `<data>` blocks; screenshots pass through the photo road (8 MB max, real JPEG/PNG first bytes, private temp folder, fixed name, deleted in `finally`); after `pc_windows`, `chrome_tabs` or `pc_look`, instant doing-tools are refused in that message; at most 3 looks/message and 30/hour; never on a timer or file follow-up; Bot never switches tabs, focuses, unminimizes, clicks or types (minimized/background limits are reported plainly); works from the phone too, and every look is said in the reply and logged in `data/bot.log` without content.
